How ASEEL works

ASEEL is not a perfume shop. It is an authentication layer: one unique QR tag per box, linked to a registered record that customers can compare with the physical packaging.

01

Admin registers a unit

A brand owner records the perfume name, brand, batch, unique box serial, seller, and place of sale. Batch numbers may repeat. Box serials must be unique.

02

ASEEL generates a Smart QR Tag

Each unit receives a unique verification code and a QR image that opens the public record. Print it and place it on the box or bottle.

03

The customer scans

No account is required. The customer scans the tag or types the code, then checks whether the on-screen details match the physical packaging.

04

Scan count is evidence

Every public scan is logged. After the threshold (default 5), ASEEL shows an authenticity warning and marks the unit as Suspicious, because the same code may have been copied.

Suggested 2-minute demo

  1. Open the home page and verify Royal Oud — authentic green record.
  2. Verify FAKE-123 — red Product Not Verified, logged as an invalid attempt.
  3. Log in as admin and register a new perfume. Download or print the QR.
  4. Open the new public record and watch the scan count increase.
  5. Scan Amber Night more than five times to trigger the warning and Suspicious status.
  6. Use Reset scan count if you need to replay the sequence.
